Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of eleven players. It originated in England and is now popular worldwide, especially in India, Australia, Pakistan, and the West Indies.

The game is played on a rectangular field with a pitch at the center. Two batsmen from one team face bowlers from the opposing team. The objective is to score runs by hitting the ball and running between wickets, while the fielding team tries to dismiss batsmen.

Cricket has three main formats:
1. Test Cricket - The longest format, played over five days
2. One Day Internationals (ODI) - 50 overs per side
3. Twenty20 - The shortest format with 20 overs per side

The sport combines skill, strategy, and athleticism. Players must master batting, bowling, and fielding techniques. Cricket is not just a sport but a cultural phenomenon in many countries, with millions of fans following international tournaments like the ICC World Cup and Indian Premier League.
